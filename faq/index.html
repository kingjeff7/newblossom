Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to Blossom P Restaurant, where delicious meals meet great service! Here are answers to the questions we hear most.
What is the specialty of Blossom P Restaurant?

We specialize in premium delicacies, snacks, and fast-food, all made with the freshest ingredients.

What are your opening hours and payment options?

We're open Monday to Sunday, 9:00am to 10:00pm. We accept cash, card, and digital payment methods for your convenience.

Do we take bulk orders for any event?

Yes, we do take bulk orders for events, but with prior notice.

Issues with incomplete orders?

For every order, the customer is supposed to confirm their order before leaving the restaurant.

Blossom P Restaurant and Fast-Food is a vibrant dining destination located in the heart of Benin City, Nigeria. Known for its warm hospitality and delicious offerings, the restaurant specializes in a wide range of local and international cuisines, including freshly prepared delicacies, snacks, and premium fast-food options.
